# Oval stamp

![Rendered in a hoop](feed.jpg)

[Stitch chart](chart.png) · [Detail check](detail-check.png)

- **Size:** 160 × 101 stitches. On 14-count Aida that's 11.4 × 7.2 in; on 18-count, 8.9 × 5.6 in.
- **Fabric:** cream Aida.
- **Shape match:** 0.72 (1.00 is a perfect match with the original art).

DMC matches are the closest by color math. Check them against a real DMC color card before you buy.

## What the stitching changes

- 1 thin line becomes backstitch (single-thread outlines).
- This art has more detail than cross-stitch can hold at this size. Bold, simple art with a few flat colors stitches best.
